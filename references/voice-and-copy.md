# Voice & Copy Guidelines

## Core Principles

1. **No first-person in product/tool pages**: Sub-sites, dashboards, and `about` tabs describe the problem, signals, and mechanics objectively—omit `我`, `我们`, `笔者`, `本站`. Reserve first-person strictly for signed blog posts on `Square Fold`.
2. **Explain jargon in concrete everyday actions**: Do not leave financial or technical terms (`RPO`, `DSO`, `HY OAS`) unexplained. Translate them into physical cash/contract movement a newcomer understands in one read:
   - Good: `云服务不是一手交钱一手交货，大企业通常会一次签下三五年的合同，再按月慢慢用、慢慢付钱。`
   - Bad: `RPO（Remaining Performance Obligations）是衡量云基础设施需求可见性的核心前瞻性指标。`
3. **Cut throat-clearing & meta-sections**: Do not add bloated `怎么运转 / 系统架构 / 愿景` filler sections when one sentence in a footer note suffices (e.g., `每日更新。抓取自 FRED 与能源信息署公开数据。`).
4. **Prevent AI writing tics**:
   - Avoid promotional adjectives (`极具前瞻性`, `无缝衔接`, `全方位`, `深度洞察`).
   - Avoid mechanical `不是……而是……` triads and overuse of em-dashes (`——`).
   - Keep `about` pages tight enough to read within roughly one desktop screen.
5. **Typography polish in Chinese**: Apply `text-wrap: pretty` on descriptions and prose paragraphs, and tune `max-width` or phrasing so paragraphs do not end with a lone single-character + period line (`孤字`).
